# Dr. Ankit Singh

**Assistant Professor of Mathematics** · SVKM’s College of Engineering, Shirpur, India  
**Research:** Nonlinear inverse problems · Iterative regularization · Numerical analysis

I study regularization methods for nonlinear inverse problems and ill-posed operator equations. My work focuses on iterative methods, convergence and error analysis, and parameter-choice rules.

## Research interests

- Nonlinear inverse problems and nonlinear ill-posed operator equations
- Iterated Lavrentiev regularization
- Newton and Gauss–Newton methods
- Convergence analysis and error estimates
- Heuristic parameter-choice rules
- Noise propagation and uncertainty quantification
- Numerical analysis and scientific computing

## Current research directions

- Adaptive and statistical regularization for nonlinear inverse problems
- Oracle-type convergence and error analysis under uncertain noise
- Uncertainty-aware iterative reconstruction and reliable numerical methods
